在天賦樹中試試這些變動：德魯伊、聖騎士、法師、牧師。

A hotfix to the October 30 build, first seen on October 30 in the cache the launch realms send, which Blizzard has posted no notes for. It reworks the druid and paladin talent trees and brings in the racial pass announced on October 26. Values that flip between hotfixes can come from differences between the NA and EU realms: in that case this page follows the newer hotfix.

聖騎士

  • 增強 生者與死者：This Legacy perk now makes Righteous Fury free too after you are resurrected. Its tooltip does not say so.
  • 增強 盾牌壁壘：Raises your block chance by up to 30% instead of 20%.
  • 增強 強化正義之怒：Reduces damage taken by up to 9% instead of 6%. Moves to row 4.
  • 增強 正義之怒：Now increases all threat by 33%, and the threat of your Holy attacks by another 90%, instead of 90% for Holy attacks only.
  • 增強 定罪：Has 3 ranks instead of 5: up to 6% critical strike chance with all spells and attacks, was 5% with melee attacks. Moves to the second column of row 2.
  • 增強 光之勇士：Has 5 ranks instead of 3, still up to 60% of your Intellect as spell damage, and now also takes up to 1 sec off the cast time of Hammer of Wrath.
  • 削弱 辯護：No longer raises your own attack power by 1 to 3%. It still lowers the target’s.
  • 削弱 憤怒聖印：Its Judgement taunts the target for 3 sec instead of 4.
  • 削弱 光明祝福：No longer taught by trainers.
  • 削弱 強效光明祝福：No longer taught by trainers.
  • 削弱 救贖：No longer taught by trainers.
  • 削弱 群體救贖：No longer taught by trainers.
  • 削弱 撤銷：No longer taught by trainers.
  • 調整 真理之聲：Leaves the talent tree: trainers teach it at level 20 instead.
  • 調整 神聖強化：Increases the critical healing bonus of your Holy spells by 10 to 50%, instead of giving Holy Shock and Holy Strike 3 to 15% critical strike chance and your other spells 1 to 5%.
  • 調整 神性精準：Has 2 ranks instead of 3 and no longer needs Holy Shock: up to 8% hit chance with Holy spells instead of 18%, and Silence effects on you up to 20% shorter. Moves to row 4.
  • 調整 聖光戒備：Works on friendly targets only: it no longer damages enemies. Its range is 40 yd instead of 20.
  • 調整 精神集中：Has 5 ranks instead of 2, for the same 70% at the last rank. Moves to row 1.
  • 調整 崇敬：Has 2 ranks instead of 3, for the same 30% at the last rank. Moves to row 2.
  • 調整 強化聖印：Has 5 ranks instead of 3, for the same 15% at the last rank.
  • 調整 迅捷審判：Reworked: unleashes a Seal’s Judgement without consuming the Seal, on a cooldown of its own. Now needs Templar’s Bulwark instead of Improved Seal of Fury.
  • 調整 神聖職務：No longer gives Stamina: it now also shortens Forbearance by 5 or 10 sec. Moves to row 2.
  • 調整 鐵之教條：Has 3 ranks instead of 5: up to 30% more Holy Strike threat instead of 25%, and 6% less damage taken instead of 10%.
  • 調整 盾牌專精：Has 2 ranks instead of 3: 2 ranks give what 3 did, 30% more absorbed and a 100% chance to restore Mana.
  • 調整 Unsealed Wrath：The new name of Twist of Light, reworked, and now after Repentance: your melee attacks store your active Seal for 15 sec, up to 3 times, and an attack with a different Seal consumes them, each stack getting the stored Seal’s normal chance to trigger. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 調整 雙手武器專精：Increases all your physical damage by up to 6% instead of your two-handed weapon damage, and cuts your threat by up to 20% while a two-handed weapon is equipped. Moves to the fourth column of row 4.
  • 調整 復仇：Now needs Seal of Command instead of Sanctified Judgement, and moves to the third column of row 5.
  • 調整 Oath of Light新天賦：New talent in row 4, where Voice of Truth was: swear an Oath on an ally for 5 min, and your Holy Light, Flash of Light and Holy Shock heal them for more. The server sets the amounts, which the data does not carry yet. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 調整 Boundless Light新天賦：New talent in row 5, after Holy Shock, 2 ranks: up to 30% more critical strike chance for Holy Shock on allies without your Oath of Light. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 調整 Stoicism新天賦：New talent in row 5, 2 ranks: Stun and Charm effects on you are up to 20% shorter. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 調整 Shieldsworn新天賦：New talent in row 7, after Holy Shield: Holy Strike’s cooldown is 4 sec shorter, and Holy Strike hits with your shield, setting off Holy Shield and Shield Specialization without using their charges or cooldowns, and lets you block spells for 6 sec. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 調整 Turn Evil新天賦：New talent in row 5, 2 ranks: Turn Undead casts up to 1 sec faster with up to 10 sec less cooldown, and works on Demons. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 削弱 單手武器專精已移除：Removed from the talent tree: it gave up to 10% more damage with one-handed weapons.
  • 調整 強化憤怒聖印已移除：已從天賦樹中移除。
  • 調整 法之公器已移除：Removed from the talent tree: Champion of the Light takes its Hammer of Wrath cast time and Two-Handed Weapon Specialization its threat cut.
  • 調整 啟發已移動：Moves from row 4 to row 3, still under Reverence, which now has 2 ranks.
  • 調整 神聖導體已移動：Moves from the Retribution tree to Holy, in the third column of row 3.
  • 調整 神聖智慧已移動：Moves from the Holy tree to Protection, in the second column of row 1.
  • 調整 不滅信仰已移動：Moves from the Holy tree to Retribution, in the second column of row 3.
  • 調整 神聖之盾已移動：Moves from row 7 to row 5 and no longer needs Templar’s Bulwark. A new rank 1 is learned at level 30 (75 damage per block, 100 Mana), the old ranks follow at levels 40 and 50 (110 and 153 damage), and the 221 damage rank has no level in the data yet.
  • 調整 神聖之力已移動：Moves from the second to the third column of row 1.
  • 調整 淨化能量已移動：Moves from row 3 to row 2, in the fourth column.
  • 調整 神恩術已移動：Moves from row 4 to row 3, in the first column.
  • 調整 聖光灌注已移動：Moves from the first to the second column of row 4.
  • 調整 神聖之地已移動：Moves from the third to the first column of row 5.
  • 調整 堅韌已移動：從第 1 排移到第 3 排，位於第 4 欄。
  • 調整 聖殿騎士之壁壘已移動：Moves from row 5 to row 3, in the first column.
  • 調整 強化裁決之錘已移動：Moves from row 4 to row 3, in the second column.
  • 調整 預知已移動：Moves from row 2 to row 4, in the third column.
  • 調整 聖化審判已移動：Moves from row 3 to row 2, in the third column.
  • 調整 神聖仲裁者已移動：Moves from the third to the second column of row 4.
  • 調整 懺悔已移動：Moves from the third to the second column of row 5.

獵人

  • 調整 尋路已移動：Moves from row 2 to row 3, in the first column.
  • 調整 強化復活寵物已移動：Moves from the fourth to the third column of row 2.

牧師

  • 增強 祛病術：Can now be cast in Shadowform.
  • 增強 驅除疾病：Can now be cast in Shadowform.
  • 削弱 暗言術：死：Its backlash deals 30% of your maximum health instead of 10%.
  • 調整 束縛治療：Leaves the talent tree: trainers teach rank 1 at level 25 (236 to 284, 155 Mana), and ranks 2 to 6 as before.
  • 調整 Holy Binding新天賦：New talent in row 4, where Binding Heal was: when your Flash Heal heals someone else, you are healed for 50% as much. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 調整 嶄新希望：Its tooltip no longer names Binding Heal.

法師

  • 增強 冰霜長矛：Deals 300% more damage to Frozen targets instead of 200%, at every rank.
  • 增強 寒冰護體：Increases your Frost damage by 15% for 1 min instead of 3%, and by another 5% instead of 3% while the shield holds.
  • 增強 深冬之寒：Each stack makes the target take 20% more damage from your next Cone of Cold instead of 5%.
  • 增強 極寒延伸：Now extends the range of Ice Lance too, by 10% or 20%.
  • 增強 秘法轉化：Has 3 ranks instead of 2: up to 75% of your Intellect as Armor, was 50%.
  • 削弱 冰霜長矛：Ranks 2 to 6 now have the 8 sec cooldown of rank 1 too. Only rank 1, the talent’s, had it.
  • 調整 秘法衝擊：Each cast gives 12% of your Intellect as spell damage instead of 15% and raises its mana cost by 100% instead of 175%. The effect lasts 18 sec instead of 15.

術士

  • 調整 強化吸取：Now covers Drain Mana instead of Wrack: 7 to 20% more Mana drained.

德魯伊

  • 增強 生生不息：Increases your periodic damage and healing by 2 to 10% instead of 1 to 5%.
  • 增強 強化治療之觸：Increases all damage you deal by 2 to 10% instead of 1 to 5%, and now cuts the cast time of Wild Growth by 0.1 to 0.5 sec too, as it does Healing Touch’s.
  • 增強 梟獸形態：Its aura now raises every kind of Critical Strike chance of the party or raid by 3%, not only spell critical strikes.
  • 增強 獸群領袖：Its aura now raises every kind of Critical Strike chance of the party or raid by 3%, spells included, not only melee and ranged.
  • 增強 兇猛撕咬：A new rank 1 is trained at level 24, 8 levels sooner: 33 to 43 damage with 1 combo point, up to 129 to 139 with 5. The old ranks become ranks 2 to 6.
  • 削弱 野性痊癒：Has a 3 sec cast instead of being instant. Naturalist can bring it down to 2.5 sec.
  • 調整 割裂：Now a talent in row 4, under Savage Fury, where Primal Bite was. The talent teaches a new rank 1 (20 damage over 15 sec), trainers a new rank 2 at level 34, and the old ranks follow as ranks 3 to 5 at levels 42, 50 and 58.
  • 調整 Predatory Discipline新天賦：New talent in row 4, third column: Ferocious Bite no longer spends your extra Energy, but still deals extra damage for it. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 調整 Bloom新天賦：New talent in row 3, where Gift of the Earthmother was: when your Rejuvenation or Regrowth runs out, the target is healed for 100% of your Spirit, or 50% if it was dispelled. Not yet translated in the game.
  • 削弱 狂暴已移除：Removed from the talent tree: Primal Bite takes its place in row 7.
  • 調整 強化能量流轉已移除：Removed from the talent tree: it took 4 or 8 sec off the cooldown of Shifting Power, which is now 8 sec.
  • 調整 大地之母賜福已移除：Removed from the talent tree: it took 0.5 sec off the global cooldown of Rejuvenation, Swiftmend and Wild Growth. Bloom takes its place.
  • 調整 原始撕咬已移動：Moves from row 4 to row 7, under Leader of the Pack, so it comes at level 40 instead of 25. It hits harder and has 3 ranks instead of 4, the next two trained at levels 50 and 60: plus 42, 62 and 77 damage, was 26, 38, 59 and 77.
  • 調整 能量流轉已移動：Moves from row 4 to row 7, third column, under Predatory Discipline. Its cooldown is 8 sec instead of 16.
  • 調整 激怒已移動：Moves to the Feral Combat tree, in the third column of row 1, where Heart of the Wild was.
  • 調整 野性之心已移動：Moves to the Restoration tree, in the third column of row 1, where Furor was.
  • 調整 猛獸攻擊已移動：Moves from the third to the first column of row 4.
  • 調整 碎裂撕扯已移動：Moves from the third to the first column of row 6, still under Predatory Strikes.
  • 調整 自然反應已移動：Moves from the first to the fourth column of row 6.
  • 調整 反射已移動：Moves from row 3 to row 2, where Subtlety was.
  • 調整 微妙已移動：Moves from row 2 to row 3, where Reflection was.
  • 調整 迅癒：Its tooltip now says it needs your own Rejuvenation or Regrowth and consumes the one it uses. Only the wording changed.

人類

  • 增強 人類精魂：Raises Spirit by 10% instead of 5%, as announced for launch.
  • 削弱 無情：Gives 1% critical strike chance instead of 2%, as announced for launch.

獸人

  • 增強 野蠻：Replaced by Command: Hunter and Warlock pets deal 5% more damage, instead of 1% critical strike chance, as announced for launch.

矮人

  • 增強 大地怒靈：Replaced by Artisan’s Precision: 1% hit chance with spells and attacks instead of 2% more critical damage and healing, as announced for launch. Find Treasure now also says it sometimes increases the treasure found.

不死族

  • 增強 墓穴之觸：Its internal cooldown is 15 sec instead of 25, for casters and for Paladins, Rogues and Warriors alike.

牛頭人

  • 增強 原野疾馳：Each stack gives 2% movement speed instead of 1%, up to 60% instead of 30%, and it no longer adds mounted speed, as announced for launch.
  • 削弱 耐久：Only the 5% Health stays: the 2% critical damage and healing bonus is gone, as announced for launch.

食人妖

  • 增強 狂暴：Lasts 15 sec instead of 10.

天裔精靈

  • 削弱 風之祝福：Gives 2% haste instead of 3%, as announced for launch.
  • 調整 解讀地脈：Raises Health and Mana regeneration by 50% for 15 sec. At a leyline you become Attuned for 15 min, which doubles it, as announced for launch.
  • 調整 天之視覺：Gives 15% movement speed for 30 sec. At an elemental convergence you become Charged for 15 min, which doubles it, as announced for launch.
