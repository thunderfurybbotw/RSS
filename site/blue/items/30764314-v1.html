Hi There,

We are aware of some issues with transports such as boats and zeppelins becoming lost in the maelstrom for extended periods of time.

As a temporary fix, we’ve spawned portals near most transport docks or inside of the zeppelin towers to help you move between continents if your boat gets lost. We have a proper fix going out in the morning, and once we’ve verified that’s in and working, we’ll be removing those portals.

Hope this helps. Thank you!
