Added to the list above:

  • The Cooldown Manager is a work in progress. Complete implementation varies from class to class.
  • Armor bonuses from consumables and buffs are multiplied by Bear Form armor bonuses.
  • Queueing an On-Next Attack ability (Heroic Strike/Cleave/Raptor Strike) disables your spell queue window.
  • Taking damage while channeling Arcane Missiles will fire off a full damage bolt instead of a bolt with truncated damage.
  • Resurrection sickness’s duration is not correct at lower levels.
