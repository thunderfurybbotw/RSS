With additional hotfixes that are now live, we have further increased the amount of reputation gained from defeating Lieutenants and holding graveyards and mines at the end of long matches, and we’ve shortened the threshold for a “long match” bonus reputation to 20 minutes (was 60 minutes in Era and prior to today’s adjustments).

The overall intent for all of these changes was for games to feel more meaningful while trying to maintain a similar amount of rep gained over a similar play time within the battleground, even with longer match lengths. There will be fluctuations depending on how successful you are at destroying towers and holding capture points at the end of a match however.

These changes will be included in today’s hotfixes update along with those listed in the OP.
