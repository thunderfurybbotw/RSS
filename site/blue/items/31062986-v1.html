[Kaivax] Alterac Valley Adjustments - November 25

2026年11月25日 · 在暴雪论坛查看原帖

Kaivax（Community Manager） 回复于第 1 楼

We’ve seen that players in Alterac Valley were frequently completing the battleground without capturing the objectives, so we’ve made some adjustments to incentivize a focus on the objectives rather than rushing to kill the enemy faction’s General. The intent with these adjustments is for Honor gains to be relatively the same and reputation to be increased per match. Additionally, the difficulty of enemy creatures inside Alterac Valley has been lower than intended, so we’ve increased that, and we’ve made some adjustments to how the Warmasters/Marshals and Faction Generals behave to improve their sturdiness.

Player versus Player

  • Alterac Valley
    • Reputation and Honor from killing the enemy faction General has been significantly reduced. The reputation from objectives (Towers, Graveyards, and Mines) has been increased to compensate. Honor from these objectives has been slightly increased as a redistribution.
    • Reputation from killing enemy NPCs has been increased.
    • Damage and Health has been increased for all enemy creatures in Alterac Valley.
    • Warmasters/Marshals will now leash if they leave their respective rooms, and they now aggro from further away.
    • Generals and Warmasters/Marshals all are stronger if the objectives are present. This buff lasts for 20 minutes and starts with 4 stacks. Each objective taken removes a stack.
    • Generals now deal more damage.
    • Fixed a bug where some Generals were not as powerfully defensive as intended.
    • Generals now have increased stealth detection.
